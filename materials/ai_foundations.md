# Artificial Intelligence Foundations

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence by machines. It includes systems that can reason, learn, and solve problems using data and algorithms.

Machine learning is a subset of AI that allows systems to improve automatically through experience. Models are trained with data, then used to make predictions or classify new information.

Deep learning uses neural networks with multiple layers to discover patterns in large data sets. It is especially useful for image recognition, audio processing, and natural language understanding.

A study plan should begin with core definitions, then move into supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and practical projects. Frequent review and active recall help reinforce the concepts.
